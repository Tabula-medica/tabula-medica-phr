## Before your appointment, bring

- Government photo ID (passport preferred) and, if you have one, your A-number
- **All vaccination records**, in any language (childhood card, school records, prior I-693, WHO yellow card)
- Any recent TB test result (IGRA / QuantiFERON / T-SPOT) or chest X-ray report
- Records of past treatment for TB, syphilis, or other conditions listed on the form
- Your attorney's name and email so we can confirm completion

## What happens

1. **Exam day (45–60 min):** history, physical, TB blood draw, syphilis and gonorrhea tests (age-based), vaccine review. Missing vaccines are given the same day when medically appropriate.
2. **Lab results:** usually 2–3 business days.
3. **Pickup:** you receive the **sealed** I-693 envelope (do not open it) plus a copy for your records. We notify your attorney it's ready.
