## Rationale for $299 Anchor

**Why $299/mo annual (not $249 or $399):**

1. **SNF willingness-to-pay signal:** Survey-related deficiencies cost SNFs far more than $299/mo in staff time, consultant fees, and POC remediation. A single $3,000+ civil money penalty exceeds a year of RegLens. The price anchors well below the pain point.

2. **Below PCC/MatrixCare psychology:** Most SNF decision-makers are conditioned to $500–800/mo EHR contracts. At $299, RegLens reads as an inexpensive add-on rather than a major budget line.

3. **Above-floor credibility:** Generic compliance tools at $150–200/mo signal "checkbox" software. $299 signals a professional clinical tool without crossing the "needs a budget meeting" threshold for a DON or Administrator signing authority.

4. **Annual commitment converts trials:** The 2-months-free framing on annual ($2,988 vs. $4,188 monthly equivalent) creates clear urgency at the end of the 30-day trial without a hard pitch.
