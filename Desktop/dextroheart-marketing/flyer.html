DextroHeart
The first portable all-in-one cardiac platform — 14-lead ECG, sternal SCG/PCG, and real-time ultrasound on a single tablet.
Wireless BLE iPad & Android FHIR R4 Export AI-Assisted Analysis IEC 62304 Class B
⚡
14-Lead ECG
Standard + right-heart + posterior leads. 500 Hz, 24-bit ADC, BLE streaming. Five acquisition profiles:
Standard 12 Right 14 Posterior 15 Legacy 14 Combined 17
🔊
Sternal SCG/PCG
Mid-sternal patch captures seismocardiogram (mechanical cardiac motion) and phonocardiogram (heart sounds) simultaneously. PEP, LVET, S1/S2/S3/S4 auto-detected. No gel required.
🖼
Cardiac Ultrasound
Apical-4C / 2C cine capture via USB-C transducer. Simpson biplane EF, M-mode LVIDs/LVIDd, Doppler valve analysis (aortic & mitral Vmax, VTI, E/A, DT, PHT).
