Dear Cardiologist / EP Lab Director,

We are launching DextroHeart — the first portable all-in-one cardiac
platform that captures:

  ✓ 14-Lead ECG (5 profiles: Standard 12 / Right 14 / Posterior 15 /
    Legacy 14 / Combined 17)
  ✓ Sternal Seismocardiogram (SCG) + Phonocardiogram (PCG)
    — PEP, LVET, S1/S2/S3/S4 detection, no gel
  ✓ Cardiac Ultrasound — EF (Simpson biplane), M-mode, Doppler
    (Vmax, VTI, E/A, PHT for aortic and mitral valves)

All from a single Bluetooth patch + USB-C transducer.
Controlled from an iPad or Android tablet app.
FHIR R4 export. Open Python SDK.
