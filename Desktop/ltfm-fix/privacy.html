Privacy Policy

Lansdowne Travel & Family Medicine, LLC · Last updated: September 2026

Lansdowne Travel & Family Medicine, LLC ("we," "us," or "our"), operated by Dr. Rajiv K. Aggarwal, MD, is a HIPAA-covered healthcare entity. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit ltfm.health or use our patient services.

1. Protected Health Information (PHI)

Medical records, diagnoses, treatment notes, and other information about your health are Protected Health Information under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA). We maintain a separate Notice of Privacy Practices that governs how we handle PHI — available at the front desk or upon request.

2. Information Collected on This Website

  • Contact & Appointment Requests: Name, email, phone, and message content you submit through contact or appointment forms.
  • Usage Data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, and access times (collected automatically for security and analytics).

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Respond to appointment requests and patient inquiries.
  • Provide primary care, travel medicine, and related healthcare services.
  • Comply with legal obligations including HIPAA, Virginia state health laws, and CMS requirements.
  • Maintain the security and integrity of this website.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. PHI is shared only as permitted or required by HIPAA (treatment, payment, healthcare operations, or legal mandate). Website inquiry data is not combined with your medical record.

5. Data Security

Electronic PHI is encrypted at rest and in transit. Access is restricted to authorized clinical staff. We perform regular risk assessments consistent with the HIPAA Security Rule.

6. Your Rights

  • HIPAA Rights (Patients): You have the right to access, amend, and request restrictions on your PHI. See our Notice of Privacy Practices for details.
  • Website Data: To request deletion of information you submitted through this website, email privacy@ltfm.health.
  • California (CCPA): California residents may request disclosure and deletion of personal information we hold. We do not sell personal information.

7. Cookies

This site uses only essential cookies required for security and session management. No advertising or tracking cookies are used.
