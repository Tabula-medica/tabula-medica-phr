# 01 — Hero loop: the stone in the dark

**Use:** hero background, muted, seamless-ish loop · **Duration:** 8 s · **Ratio:** 16:9 · **Resolution:** 720p web (1080p master) · **Audio:** off
**References:** optional `@Image1` = the site's diamond render or logo (palette and cut reference only).

## Prompt

【Style】Museum documentary, macro cinematography, near-black background with a single warm shaft of light as if from a mine opening, deep indigo and gold palette, ultra-clean, no text, no logos, no people.
【Duration】8 seconds, designed to loop: the final frame composition matches the opening frame.

[00:00-00:03] Shot 1: Extreme close-up of a large oval-cut diamond resting on dark stone, edges catching a slow warm light from the upper left. Dust motes drift. Camera holds fixed.
[00:03-00:06] Shot 2 (continuous, no cut): The diamond rotates a slow quarter turn; internal facets flare briefly in gold, then settle to cool white.
[00:06-00:08] Shot 3 (continuous): The light dims back toward the opening state as the rotation completes, so the loop restarts invisibly.

Camera: fixed, completely still, shallow depth of field. Motion is slow, smooth, stable, gradual.
Avoid: text, letters, numbers, logos, hands, faces, crowns or royal regalia, jitter, temporal flicker, fast motion, lens flare streaks, camera shake.
