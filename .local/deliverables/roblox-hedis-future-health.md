# Future Health — HEDIS-concept learning for kids (Roblox)

Status: MVP shipped on branch `claude/roblox-healthcare-games-i0fz76` (PR #106
follow-up). Companion to `roblox-healthcare-games-concept.md`.

## The ask, and the reframing

Ask: "a healthcare game on Roblox where physicians and hospitals are rated on
HEDIS measures using AI, no PHI, futuristic, makes children health-savvy."

Rating **real** physicians/hospitals inside a children's game is the one part
we deliberately did not build, and recommend never building into this
experience:

| Risk | Why it bites |
|---|---|
| Defamation / provider relations | An AI-derived score on a named clinician or hospital that's wrong (or just contested) is a lawsuit and a lost partner. Kids can't evaluate it; it's not education for them. |
| NCQA licensing | HEDIS® is NCQA's trademarked, licensed measure set. Publishing "HEDIS ratings" without certified measure logic and a license is a trademark/contract problem, not a technical one. |
| Small-denominator re-identification | Provider-level rates on pediatric sub-populations can re-identify patients — PHI exposure even from "aggregate" data. |
| Platform fit | COPPA-scoped Roblox experiences are the wrong venue for adult healthcare quality politics. |

The provider-facing version of this idea (AI-assisted HEDIS gap analysis for
practices/plans, on de-identified claims, with licensed measure logic) is a
legitimate **adult B2B feature for Tabula Medica proper** and is out of scope
here — filed as a follow-up below.

What we built instead keeps the *educational payload* of HEDIS — "this is what
good preventive care looks like, and why" — and applies the rating to the
**player's own pretend clinic**, using AI only for coaching language.

## The game

**Premise.** The player runs a bright, futuristic pediatric clinic with an AI
guide, **Dr. Nova**. Fictional NPC patients arrive with a *care gap* modeled on
a HEDIS measure concept. The player has a short window to walk over and "do
the visit." Close enough gaps and the clinic earns stars; Dr. Nova coaches
between visits.

**Measure catalog (kid-safe subset).** Defined in
`server/roblox-clinic-routes.ts` → `CLINIC_MEASURES`. Adult-only screenings
are excluded on purpose.

| In-game name | Teaches | HEDIS concept it's modeled on |
|---|---|---|
| Yearly Checkup | Checkups happen even when you feel fine | Child & Adolescent Well-Care Visits (WCV) |
| Shots on Time | Vaccines protect you and your friends | Childhood Immunization Status (CIS) / IMA |
| Move & Fuel Check | Growth, food and play get checked every visit | Weight Assessment & Counseling (WCC) |
| Breathe-Easy Plan | Everyday controller medicine for asthma | Asthma Medication Ratio (AMR) |
| Pressure Check | What the arm cuff is for | Controlling High Blood Pressure (CBP) |
| Safe-Home Test | Lead hides in old paint/pipes; a tiny test checks | Lead Screening in Children (LSC) |
| Smile Check | Dentist twice a year | Oral Evaluation, Dental Services (OED) |
| Feelings Check-In | Doctors ask how you feel inside, too | Depression Screening & Follow-Up (DSF-E) |

Each entry carries an `npcPrompt` (the line the NPC says — "I feel great, do I
still need my checkup?") and a `whatItTeaches` line shown on completion. That
question-then-explanation loop is the whole pedagogy: the child hears the
common excuse, then the reason it's wrong.

**Scoring — the "AI rates the clinic" part, done honestly.**
- Rate per measure = closed ÷ (closed + missed), exactly how HEDIS-style
  measures are expressed (numerator/denominator), so kids absorb the shape of
  a quality measure without the jargon.
- Overall stars (1–5) are **rules-based** from the overall rate (≥90% → 5,
  ≥75% → 4, ≥60% → 3, ≥40% → 2), echoing the Star Ratings mental model.
  Deterministic on purpose: a score a kid can predict is a score they can
  learn from.
- **AI is used for coaching language only.** Dr. Nova's tip is generated by
  the existing BAA-covered provider path (`services/ai-provider.ts`,
  Vertex by default, fail-closed) from *aggregate game counts only* — no name,
  no PHI, no free text from the child. Hard-scoped system prompt (kids 7–12,
  ≤2 sentences, no medical advice, no medication names, no real providers,
  never ask for personal info), length-capped, and it falls back to a curated
  tip bank if AI is unconfigured or errors. Tips are cached per scorecard
  state so the game doesn't hammer the model.
- ≥10 visits at ≥4 stars auto-awards the `roblox-checkup-champion` badge
  through the same PHI-free sync as the other mini-games, so it appears in the
  app's Achievements → Roblox tab.

**Futuristic dressing (Studio build guidance, not code).** Holographic
"body-scan" table for Pressure Check and Move & Fuel; a floating immunization
timeline for Shots on Time; Dr. Nova as a hovering drone-companion with the
tip panel; star rating rendered as the clinic's sign lighting up. None of the
theming touches data flow.

## What shipped

Backend (`server/roblox-clinic-routes.ts`, mounted at `/api/roblox/clinic/*`):
- `GET /measures` (server key) — catalog for the Roblox server
- `POST /event` (server key) — `{robloxUserId, measureId, outcome}` → updated scorecard
- `GET /scorecard?robloxUserId=` (server key)
- `GET /me` (app session) — the family sees the kid's clinic in the app
- `roblox-education-link-routes.ts` now exports `awardRobloxBadge` /
  `getLinkedPatientId` / `requireRobloxApiKey` / `getPatientId` so the clinic
  module reuses the same link + reward store; `/rewards/sync` refactored onto
  the shared helper.

Roblox (`roblox/src/…`):
- `MiniGames/FutureHealth.server.lua` — round loop: NPC gets a random
  measure, `VisitPrompt` enabled for 45s, closed/missed posted, scorecard
  pushed to the client. Scene contract documented at the top of the script.
- `BadgeSync.lua` — `FetchClinicMeasures`, `RecordClinicEvent`,
  `FetchClinicScorecard`.
- `RemoteEvents.lua` — `ClinicScorecardUpdated`.
- `ClientUI.client.lua` — Dr. Nova panel (stars + tip).

App (`client/src/pages/gamification-dashboard.tsx`): Future Health card
on the Roblox tab — stars, visit count, Champion badge, Dr. Nova tip,
per-measure progress bars, with the "these are not certified HEDIS® measures"
disclaimer in the card description.

## Follow-ups (not in this branch)

1. **Adult B2B: AI-assisted HEDIS gap analysis for practices/plans.** Needs:
   NCQA measure license or certified vendor logic, de-identified claims/FHIR
   inputs, provider consent/contract, adult-only UI in Tabula Medica proper.
   Separate deliverable; do not route through anything under `/api/roblox`.
2. Persist `clinicEvents` (DataStore on the Roblox side for offline stars;
   `storage.ts` on ours) — currently in-memory like the rest of the module.
3. Content review of `CLINIC_MEASURES` and the Nova system prompt by a
   pediatric clinician + a child-safety reviewer before public publish.
4. Trademark: keep "HEDIS®" attribution + "not certified measures" language
   in any store listing or in-game text that names it.
