# SAWD — Pilot Invite Pack

Warm, founder-to-early-user copy for the no-card real-data trial on sawd.ai. Claims are limited to
what the product actually backs (CPA-signed returns, no-card start, read-only bank link, you own /
can delete your data) — no refund or tax-outcome promises. Pricing matches `sawd-web/src/config/pricing.js`.

Placeholders in `[brackets]`.
