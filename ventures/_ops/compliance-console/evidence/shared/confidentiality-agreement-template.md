### 1. Definition of Confidential Information

1.1 "Confidential Information" means any and all non-public information disclosed by the Company to Recipient, whether orally, in writing, electronically, or by any other means, that is designated as confidential or that reasonably should be understood to be confidential given the nature of the information and the circumstances of disclosure, including without limitation:

(a) **Technical information:** source code, software, algorithms, system architecture, infrastructure configurations, API keys, credentials, authentication tokens, encryption keys, and security controls;

(b) **Business information:** business plans, financial information, pricing, customer lists, vendor relationships, contracts, and strategic plans;

(c) **Personal information:** personally identifiable information (PII) of employees, contractors, customers, or users, including names, contact information, financial data, and government identifiers;

(d) **Protected Health Information (PHI):** any individually identifiable health information as defined under 45 CFR 160.103, including but not limited to patient records, clinical data, diagnoses, treatment information, and claims data maintained by or on behalf of Tabula Medica LLC for its ventures (Uninsurance, Tabula Medica PHR, WorldEHR/omnihealth, Tabula Medica ACO, Tabula Cognita, Tabula Attentiva, and LTFM Health);

(e) **Tax and financial data:** tax return information, EFIN credentials, financial account data, and any information subject to IRS regulations; and

(f) **Any information that Recipient knows or has reason to know is confidential to the Company.**

1.2 Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of Recipient; (b) was rightfully known by Recipient before disclosure by the Company; (c) is independently developed by Recipient without use of Confidential Information; or (d) is required to be disclosed by law, regulation, or court order, provided Recipient gives prompt written notice to the Company.

---

### 2. Permitted Uses

2.1 Recipient may use Confidential Information solely for the purpose of performing services for the Company as described in the applicable engagement agreement, statement of work, or contractor agreement ("Permitted Purpose").

2.2 Recipient shall not use Confidential Information for any other purpose, including without limitation for Recipient's own benefit, to compete with the Company, or to develop products or services similar to the Company's products.

2.3 **Prohibition on AI Processing of PHI:** Recipient shall not input, upload, submit, or otherwise transmit any PHI or PII received from or on behalf of the Company to any artificial intelligence system, large language model, machine learning platform, or AI-as-a-Service provider that does not have a signed Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with Recipient or the Company. This prohibition includes but is not limited to OpenAI, Anthropic Claude API (non-BAA plans), and consumer AI chat tools.

---

### 3. Non-Disclosure Obligations

3.1 Recipient agrees to:

(a) Hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence;

(b) Not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without the prior written consent of the Company;

(c) Limit disclosure of Confidential Information within Recipient's organization to those individuals who have a need to know for the Permitted Purpose and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Agreement;

(d) Use the same degree of care to protect the Company's Confidential Information as Recipient uses to protect its own confidential information of similar sensitivity, but in no event less than reasonable care; and

(e) Promptly notify the Company in writing upon discovery of any unauthorized access to, disclosure of, or use of Confidential Information.

3.2 **PHI Obligations.** If Recipient will access, create, receive, maintain, or transmit PHI on behalf of the Company, Recipient acknowledges that it is acting as a Business Associate (as defined under HIPAA) and agrees to comply with the requirements of 45 CFR Part 164, including:

(a) Not using or disclosing PHI except as permitted by this Agreement or required by law;

(b) Using appropriate safeguards, including administrative, physical, and technical safeguards as required by the HIPAA Security Rule, to prevent unauthorized use or disclosure of PHI;

(c) Reporting to the Company any use or disclosure of PHI not permitted by this Agreement, including any Security Incident (as defined under 45 CFR 164.304) and any Breach of Unsecured PHI (as defined under 45 CFR 164.402), within five (5) business days of discovery;

(d) Ensuring that any subcontractors who create, receive, maintain, or transmit PHI on Recipient's behalf agree to the same restrictions and conditions that apply to Recipient under this Agreement; and

(e) Returning or destroying all PHI upon termination of the engagement, unless retention is required by law.

---

### 4. Return and Destruction of Confidential Information

4.1 Upon termination or expiration of the engagement, or upon written request by the Company, Recipient shall promptly:

(a) Return all Confidential Information and copies thereof to the Company; or

(b) Destroy all Confidential Information and copies thereof, and certify such destruction in writing to the Company within ten (10) business days.

4.2 **PHI:** Return or destruction of PHI shall occur within sixty (60) days of termination. If return or destruction is infeasible, Recipient shall notify the Company and extend protections for as long as PHI is retained, consistent with 45 CFR 164.504(e)(2)(ii)(J).

4.3 Notwithstanding the foregoing, Recipient may retain one archival copy of Confidential Information (excluding PHI) solely for legal compliance purposes, subject to the confidentiality obligations of this Agreement.

---

### 5. Duration of Obligations

5.1 **Protected Health Information (PHI):** Confidentiality obligations with respect to PHI are **indefinite** and survive termination of this Agreement, consistent with HIPAA requirements.

5.2 **PII and financial/tax data:** Confidentiality obligations with respect to PII and financial/tax data survive for a period of **five (5) years** following termination or expiration of this Agreement, or until such information no longer qualifies as Confidential Information, whichever is later.

5.3 **All other Confidential Information:** Confidentiality obligations survive for a period of **three (3) years** following termination or expiration of this Agreement, or until such information no longer qualifies as Confidential Information, whichever is later.

---

### 6. Intellectual Property

6.1 Nothing in this Agreement grants Recipient any ownership interest, license, or other right in any Confidential Information except the limited right to use it for the Permitted Purpose.

6.2 All work product, inventions, and deliverables created by Recipient in the course of performing services for the Company are the exclusive property of the Company, as further specified in any applicable contractor or employment agreement.

---

### 7. Injunctive Relief

7.1 Recipient acknowledges that unauthorized disclosure or use of Confidential Information would cause irreparable harm to the Company for which monetary damages would be inadequate, and that the Company shall be entitled to seek injunctive or other equitable relief in addition to any other remedies available at law.

---

### 8. HIPAA-Specific Requirements for Business Associates

8.1 If Recipient qualifies as a Business Associate under HIPAA, the following additional terms apply and supplement Section 3.2 above:

(a) **Minimum Necessary:** Recipient shall request and use only the minimum necessary PHI to accomplish the Permitted Purpose.

(b) **Individual Rights:** Recipient shall cooperate with the Company in providing individuals access to their PHI, amending PHI, and providing an accounting of disclosures, as required by the HIPAA Privacy Rule.

(c) **Government Access:** Recipient shall make its internal practices, books, and records relating to the use and disclosure of PHI available to the Secretary of Health and Human Services for compliance purposes.

(d) **Subcontractors:** Recipient shall obtain a Business Associate Agreement from any subcontractor that will create, receive, maintain, or transmit PHI on Recipient's behalf before allowing such subcontractor to access PHI.

(e) **Breach Notification:** Recipient shall notify the Company of any Breach of Unsecured PHI within five (5) business days of discovery, including the information required by 45 CFR 164.410.

8.2 The parties agree that this Section 8 constitutes a Business Associate Agreement for purposes of HIPAA, and that in the event of a conflict between this Agreement and the Company's HIPAA policies, the more protective requirement shall govern.

---

### 9. Sanctions for Violations

9.1 Violations of this Agreement may result in:

(a) Immediate termination of the engagement without notice or compensation for remaining work;

(b) Civil liability for damages arising from unauthorized disclosure or use of Confidential Information;

(c) For PHI-related violations: civil monetary penalties under HIPAA (up to $1,900,000 per violation category per year) and referral to the Department of Health and Human Services Office for Civil Rights; and

(d) Criminal prosecution for willful violations under 45 CFR 164 or applicable state law.

---

10.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the **Commonwealth of Virginia**, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

10.2 Any dispute arising under this Agreement shall be resolved exclusively in the state or federal courts located in Fairfax County, Virginia, and each party consents to the personal jurisdiction of such courts.

11.2 **Amendment.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties.

11.3 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force.

11.4 **Waiver.** No failure to exercise any right under this Agreement shall constitute a waiver of future enforcement of such right.
