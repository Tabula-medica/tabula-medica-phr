# /deepdive

Topic: $ARGUMENTS

Structure:
1. **What it is** in two sentences.
2. **How it works**: the mechanism, step by step.
3. **History and why it exists**: the problem it solved, what came before.
4. **Competing views or alternatives** and the strongest case for each.
5. **Where it breaks**: known failure modes and edge cases.
6. **Open questions** practitioners still argue about.
7. **Sources**: primary where possible; mark anything from memory as such.

Assume an expert reader. No padding.
