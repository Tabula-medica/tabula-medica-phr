# Third-party notices

`tools/reviews.py`, `tools/themes.json` and `tools/gap.py` are adapted from
[replica-skill](https://github.com/Jakeschincariol/replica-skill) (v1.0, commit
`77c9436`), specifically `replica-entrepreneur/reviews.py`,
`replica-entrepreneur/themes.json` and `replica-diff/parity.py`.

What changed here: a competitor column and filter, health-app themes, a privacy
filter that withholds reviews containing personal identifiers, and a gap matrix
scorer that ranks adoption candidates instead of measuring clone parity. The
rest of replica-skill (recon, architect, build, backend, deploy and the others)
is not vendored and not used.
