Tabula Medica PHR

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 17, 2026  ·  Tabula Medica LLC

HIPAA Notice: Tabula Medica LLC is a Health IT developer. Where this application stores or processes protected health information (PHI) on behalf of a covered entity or business associate, we operate under a signed Business Associate Agreement (BAA) and comply with the HIPAA Privacy Rule (45 CFR Part 164). This privacy policy supplements (and does not replace) any applicable Notice of Privacy Practices issued by your healthcare provider.

1. Who We Are

Tabula Medica LLC ("Tabula Medica," "we," "us") operates the personal health record (PHR) application available at tabulamedica.us. Our mailing address is on file with Delaware Division of Corporations. Contact us at privacy@tabulamedica.com.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account information: Name, email address, and authentication credentials you provide when creating an account.
  • Health record data (PHI): Clinical data you import or that your healthcare provider shares with you — including diagnoses, medications, allergies, immunizations, lab results, vitals, and care summaries.
  • Connected records (Fasten Connect): When you use the "Connect Records" feature, you authorize us to retrieve your health records from third-party health systems via Fasten Health, Inc. We receive only the data you explicitly authorize. The connection credential is held by Fasten Health and is not stored on our servers.
  • Usage data: Log data (IP address, browser type, pages visited) for security monitoring and service improvement. This data is never combined with your PHI for advertising purposes.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To display and organize your personal health record within the application.
  • To import and deduplicate records from connected health systems.
  • To generate AI-assisted health summaries using Google Cloud Vertex AI on infrastructure subject to Google's HIPAA BAA. We do not send your PHI to any AI service without a signed BAA.
  • To operate and improve the service, including security monitoring and debugging.
  • We never sell your health data. We do not use your PHI for advertising.

4. How We Share Your Information

  • Healthcare providers: With your explicit consent, to support care coordination.
  • Service providers (subprocessors): Google Cloud Platform (infrastructure and AI, under BAA), Fasten Health (health record connectivity), Firebase/Google Identity Platform (authentication). Each subprocessor is bound by data processing agreements.
  • Legal requirements: If required by applicable law, court order, or regulatory authority.
  • We do not share your PHI with any third party for marketing or commercial purposes.

5. Health Record Connectivity (Fasten Connect)

The "Connect Records" feature uses Fasten Health, Inc. to retrieve your clinical data from participating health systems and insurance portals. When you initiate a connection:

  • You are redirected to Fasten's hosted consent interface, where you log in to your health system directly. Tabula Medica never receives your health-system password.
  • Fasten Health returns a standardized FHIR data package to us. We import and store this data in your PHR under your account.
  • Fasten Health's privacy practices are governed by their own privacy policy. Tabula Medica is not responsible for Fasten Health's data handling.
  • You may disconnect a source at any time from within the app.

6. Data Retention

We retain your health record data for as long as your account is active, or as required by applicable law. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting privacy@tabulamedica.com. Note that deletion requests for PHI held on behalf of a covered entity may require coordination with your healthcare provider.

7. Your Rights

Under HIPAA and applicable state law, you may have the right to:

  • Access and receive a copy of your health information.
  • Request corrections to inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request an accounting of certain disclosures of your PHI.
  • Restrict certain uses and disclosures (where applicable).
  • Receive communications about your PHI in a particular format or location.
  • File a complaint with the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services Office for Civil Rights if you believe your privacy rights have been violated.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@tabulamedica.com.

8. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+), encryption at rest, multi-factor authentication, and role-based access controls. We conduct periodic security reviews and maintain audit logs of PHI access. Despite these measures, no system is completely secure; please notify us immediately at security@tabulamedica.com if you suspect unauthorized access to your account.

9. Children

This service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via email or an in-app notice at least 30 days before they take effect. Your continued use of the service after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
