// Savings Modules

Your Health Wallet.
Optimized.

Tabula Medica's savings modules work together to minimize your total out-of-pocket spend — whether you're insured, underinsured, or using Uninsurance mode.

💊

Cost Plus Pharmacy Module

Every prescription auto-checked against Mark Cuban Cost Plus Drugs pricing. Metformin 90-day: $4.80. AI flags when your pharmacy charges more.

🔬

Lab Route Optimizer

Before ordering labs, AI checks your Fasten Health record for duplicate tests ordered in the last 6 months. Saves hundreds per year on redundant bloodwork.

💰

HSA Smart Manager

Tracks your HSA balance. Automatically routes eligible Uninsurance visits through your HSA card. Shows real after-tax cost of each visit (2026 DPC safe harbor).

⚖️

Insurance vs. Direct Pay Optimizer

Before each visit, AI calculates whether it's cheaper to use your insurance or Uninsurance mode. Most patients with HDHPs save $3,000–$8,000/year by choosing direct pay below their deductible.

🏥

FQHC Sliding Scale Finder

Federally Qualified Health Centers must see all patients regardless of ability to pay. HRSA API surfaces the nearest FQHC instantly — free or sliding scale based on income.
