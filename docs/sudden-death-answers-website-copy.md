**Headline:** Answers, without an autopsy.

**Primary CTA:** Talk to someone now
**Secondary CTA:** How it works

## How it works (3-step summary)

1. **We come to you.** A coordinator collects a simple cheek swab and blood sample, and arranges a body scan — all within the time before burial or cremation, no incision required.
2. **We look for answers.** A genetic test, a CT scan, and (if needed) a toxicology test are reviewed alongside medical records by a cardiologist or pathologist.
3. **You get a report.** Within days for the scan, weeks for genetics — shared with you and, if applicable, the certifying physician.

## Honest expectations section (required — do not omit or soften)

**What we can find:** Inherited heart conditions, coronary blockages, aortic problems, strokes, and many overdose or toxicology causes.

**What we can't guarantee:** A definite genetic cause is found in roughly 1 in 5 to 1 in 3 of the truly unexplained cases. Some conditions — certain heart-rhythm disorders, a blood clot in the lungs, early heart attacks — are better detected by a full autopsy. We'll tell you plainly if we think that's a better option for your family.

## FAQ (starter set)

**Is this legal instead of an autopsy?**
This is not a legal autopsy and doesn't replace a medical examiner's determination when one has jurisdiction. It's an option when the ME has declined the case or released the body without autopsy.

**Will this delay burial or cremation?**
No — collection and scanning are designed to happen within the normal window before burial or cremation, typically 24-72 hours.

**Does insurance cover this?**
Not typically. We accept [payment methods TBD]. Ask your coordinator about cost if this is a concern — we don't want cost to be the reason a family goes without answers.

**What about other family members' risk?**
If we find a genetic cause, we offer cascade testing for close relatives and can help connect you with genetic counseling.
