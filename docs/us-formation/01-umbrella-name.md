# 01 · The umbrella name

You chose a neutral parent name — none of the four brands becomes the parent. That is the
right call: it keeps Katha.kids, UHR, Noorjyoti and Unraj peers rather than making three of
them subsidiaries of the fourth, and it means any one of them can spin out later without
the parent having to rename itself.

## Shortlist

All are Indic-rooted but pronounceable and spellable by a US donor, grant officer or bank
teller — which matters more than it sounds, because this name gets typed into Pay.gov,
Goodstack, Candid, your bank's onboarding form and every grant portal.

| Name | Meaning | Why it works as the parent | Watch for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Setu Foundation, Inc.** *(recommended)* | *setu* — bridge | A bridge between four programmes, and between India and the US. Four letters, unambiguous spelling, no plausible mispronunciation. | "Setu" is in commercial use (notably an Indian fintech). Different class and different sector, but clear it properly. |
| **Sootradhar Foundation, Inc.** | *sūtradhāra* — the thread-holder; the narrator who binds a performance together | The most *meaningful* fit: literally the one who holds the threads. Strong story for a grant narrative. | Eleven letters and a spelling most Americans will get wrong on first hearing. Costs you every time someone types it. |
| **Aarambh Foundation, Inc.** | *ārambh* — beginning | Clean, optimistic, unclaimed in the US non-profit space as far as ordinary search shows. | Several Indian NGOs use it; irrelevant to a US filing but relevant to search visibility. |
| **Anant Commons, Inc.** | *ananta* — endless, boundless | "Commons" signals the shared-resource posture that runs through all four programmes, and pairs well with UHR's patient-owned-records framing. | "Commons" reads slightly academic; less natural for the children's programme. |
| **Kalpa Foundation, Inc.** | *kalpa* — imagination; also an aeon | Short, warm, works across stories and culture. | Weakest fit for health records. |

**Recommendation: Setu Foundation, Inc.** It is the only one on the list that describes what
the umbrella actually *does* — connect four unlike programmes and two countries — while
staying short enough to survive a phone call.

Delaware requires the name to contain one of: Association, Company, Corporation, Club,
Foundation, Fund, Incorporated, Institute, Society, Union, Syndicate, Limited (or an
abbreviation). **Foundation** satisfies this on its own, so "Setu Foundation" is valid
without "Inc." — but include "Inc." anyway. It signals corporate form to banks and grant
portals that parse entity types.

## Clear it before you commit — four checks, about thirty minutes

1. **Delaware name availability** — the Division of Corporations name-availability search at
   `icis.corp.delaware.gov/ecorp/namereserv/namereservation.aspx`. Availability here is
   *only* about Delaware's register; it says nothing about trademark rights. You can reserve
   a name for 120 days for $75, but if you are filing within the week, skip the reservation
   and file directly.
2. **Federal trademark** — USPTO search at `tmsearch.uspto.gov`. Look at International Class
   036 (charitable fundraising), 041 (education/entertainment) and 044 (medical services).
   A live mark in one of those classes for a similar name is a real problem; a mark in an
   unrelated class usually is not.
3. **Domain** — the parent needs its own domain even though the programmes have theirs.
   Google requires the Ad Grants account to sit on a domain the organisation owns.
4. **Existing 501(c)(3)s with the same name** — IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search at
   `apps.irs.gov/app/eos`. Not a legal bar, but a same-named charity in the same space will
   cost you donations to confusion for years.

## The four programme brands are DBAs, not entities

Once the corporation exists, Katha.kids, UHR, Noorjyoti and Unraj operate as assumed
names / trade names of the single corporation. Two consequences:

- **Register the DBAs where required.** Delaware registers trade names at the county level
  (Prothonotary's office, ~$25 each). Your operating state will have its own rule. Do this
  before you accept a donation made out to a programme brand rather than the legal name —
  otherwise the bank will bounce the cheque.
- **Trademark them separately from the parent.** USPTO TEAS, $350 per class:
  Katha.kids and Unraj in Class 041; UHR in Classes 042 and 044; Noorjyoti in Class 036 or
  045. Total ~$1,400–1,750 for four marks in one class each. This is not urgent in week one,
  but `.kids` in particular is worth protecting early.

## One note on the `.kids` domain

The `.kids` registry imposes child-safety content commitments on registrants as a condition
of registration. They align with what Katha.kids should be doing anyway — and, usefully,
with what Google Ad Grants demands of your site — but read them before building the brand
on that domain, because they are contractual obligations and not merely guidelines.
