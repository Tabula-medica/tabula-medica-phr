# 06 · Employer Identification Number

**Free. Issued immediately. Do it the day the certificate comes back.**

Apply online at `irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online`.
The EIN Assistant is open **Monday–Friday, 7am–10pm Eastern**, and the session times out
after 15 minutes of inactivity with no save — have every answer ready before you start.

You do not need to file a paper Form SS-4 unless the responsible party has no SSN or ITIN
(see below).

---

## Answers, in the order the assistant asks

| Screen | Answer |
|---|---|
| What type of legal structure? | **View Additional Types** → **Other Non-Profit/Tax-Exempt Organizations** |
| Confirm your selection | Continue |
| Why are you requesting an EIN? | **Started a new business** |
| Who is the responsible party? | **Individual** — then their name and SSN/ITIN |
| Is the responsible party a member/officer? | **Yes**, and select the role |
| Where is the business physically located? | Your actual operating address — a real street address, not a PO box |
| Legal name of entity | **[[ORG_NAME]]** — exactly as on the Certificate of Incorporation, including "Inc." if you used it |
| Trade name / DBA | Leave blank here. Register programme trade names separately (`01`). |
| County and state where located | Your operating county and state |
| State where the entity is incorporated | **Delaware** |
| Date the business started | The **filing date on the Certificate of Incorporation** |
| Closing month of accounting year | **December** |
| Do you have or expect employees in the next 12 months? | **No** unless you genuinely will — answering yes opens quarterly Form 941 obligations you do not want yet |
| Does the business own a highway motor vehicle over 55,000 lbs? | No |
| Does it involve gambling? | No |
| Does it need to file Form 720? | No |
| Does it sell or manufacture alcohol, tobacco or firearms? | No |
| What does the business do? | **Other** → describe as **"Charitable and educational organization"** |
| How do you want to receive the letter? | **Receive letter online** — you get the PDF immediately |

Download the confirmation letter (**CP 575**) on the final screen and save it. The IRS will
not reissue it. If you lose it you can request a Letter 147C later, but that is a phone call
and a wait.

---

## Three things that go wrong

**The responsible party must be a natural person with an SSN or ITIN.** If the person
forming the organisation has neither, the online application will not work. You must file a
**paper Form SS-4** by fax or mail instead — expect roughly four to five weeks by fax and
longer by mail, which pushes the whole timeline out. If that applies to you, either start
the SS-4 immediately in parallel with the Delaware filing, or appoint a US-resident director
with an SSN as the responsible party. This is worth checking *today*, because it is the only
step in this pack that can silently add a month.

**One EIN per entity, ever.** Do not apply twice. Duplicate EINs for the same organisation
are a real headache to unwind, and the 1023-EZ will be filed under whichever one you use.

**The legal name must match the certificate exactly.** Goodstack, Candid, your bank and
every corporate giving portal reconcile against the IRS record. A mismatch between
"Setu Foundation" and "Setu Foundation, Inc." will cost you a verification cycle at Google.

---

## After you have it

- Add the EIN to the corporate record book.
- Note it in the organizational meeting minutes (resolution 8 in `05`).
- You now have everything needed to open the bank account and to file the 1023-EZ.
