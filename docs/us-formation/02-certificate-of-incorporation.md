# 02 · Certificate of Incorporation — Delaware nonstock corporation

**Filed with:** Delaware Division of Corporations
**Fee:** $109 for a one-page certificate, plus $9 for each additional page. Order a
**certified copy (+$50)** at the same time — your bank will ask for it, and getting it later
is a separate errand.
**Expedited service** is available for an extra fee (same-day and 24-hour tiers). Standard
turnaround is a few business days; if you are compressing the timeline, 24-hour is usually
worth it.

> Delaware publishes a fill-in "Certificate of Incorporation for an Exempt Corporation" PDF
> at `corpfiles.delaware.gov/Inc_Exempt.pdf`. **That short form does not contain the clauses
> the IRS requires.** File the long-form text below instead. This is the single most common
> reason a DIY 501(c)(3) application is rejected: the state form is sufficient for Delaware
> and insufficient for the IRS, and nobody tells you until the IRS does.

---

## Why Articles SEVENTH and EIGHTH exist

Form 1023-EZ makes you **attest under penalty of perjury** that your organizing document
already limits your purposes to those described in §501(c)(3) and already dedicates your
assets to an exempt purpose on dissolution. You are attesting to something that must be true
*at the moment you file*. If those clauses are not in the certificate, the attestation is
false and the exemption is voidable. Amending afterwards costs a fresh Delaware filing and a
credibility problem you do not want on a first application.

So they go in now, not later.

## Text to file

```
                    CERTIFICATE OF INCORPORATION
                                OF
                          [[ORG_NAME]]
              A Delaware Non-Stock, Non-Profit Corporation


FIRST:   The name of this corporation is [[ORG_NAME]].

SECOND:  The address of its registered office in the State of Delaware is
         ____________________________, in the City of ______________,
         County of ______________, Zip Code ________. The name of its
         registered agent at such address is ____________________________.

THIRD:   The nature of the business and the purposes to be conducted and
         promoted by the corporation are to engage in any lawful act or
         activity for which corporations may be organized under the General
         Corporation Law of Delaware, provided that the corporation is
         organized and shall be operated exclusively for charitable and
         educational purposes within the meaning of Section 501(c)(3) of the
         Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended, or the corresponding
         section of any future federal tax code.

         Within those limits, the corporation advances literacy,
         health-record access, community welfare, and the preservation of
         language and culture in underserved communities, providing its
         programs free of charge to the people it serves.

FOURTH:  The corporation shall not have authority to issue capital stock.
         The corporation shall have no members. The conditions of membership,
         should the Board of Directors later determine to admit members, shall
         be as stated in the Bylaws of the corporation.

FIFTH:   The name and mailing address of the incorporator is:

                  ____________________________
                  ____________________________
                  ____________________________

SIXTH:   The corporation shall have perpetual existence.

SEVENTH: No part of the net earnings of the corporation shall inure to the
         benefit of, or be distributable to, its directors, officers, or other
         private persons, except that the corporation shall be authorized and
         empowered to pay reasonable compensation for services rendered and to
         make payments and distributions in furtherance of the purposes set
         forth in Article THIRD hereof.

         No substantial part of the activities of the corporation shall be the
         carrying on of propaganda, or otherwise attempting to influence
         legislation, and the corporation shall not participate in, or
         intervene in (including the publishing or distribution of statements)
         any political campaign on behalf of or in opposition to any candidate
         for public office.

         Notwithstanding any other provision of this certificate, the
         corporation shall not carry on any other activities not permitted to
         be carried on (a) by a corporation exempt from federal income tax
         under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code, or (b) by a
         corporation, contributions to which are deductible under Section
         170(c)(2) of the Internal Revenue Code.

EIGHTH:  Upon the dissolution of the corporation, the Board of Directors
         shall, after paying or making provision for the payment of all of the
         liabilities of the corporation, dispose of all of the assets of the
         corporation exclusively for the purposes of the corporation in such
         manner, or to such organization or organizations organized and
         operated exclusively for charitable or educational purposes as shall
         at the time qualify as an exempt organization or organizations under
         Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code, as the Board of
         Directors shall determine. Any such assets not so disposed of shall
         be disposed of by the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware,
         exclusively for such purposes or to such organization or
         organizations as said Court shall determine, which are organized and
         operated exclusively for such purposes.

NINTH:   The Board of Directors shall have the power to adopt, amend or repeal
         the Bylaws of the corporation.

TENTH:   To the fullest extent permitted by the General Corporation Law of
         Delaware, a director of the corporation shall not be personally
         liable to the corporation for monetary damages for breach of
         fiduciary duty as a director.

         IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the undersigned, being the incorporator
hereinbefore named, has executed this Certificate of Incorporation this
_____ day of ______________, 20____.


                              _________________________________
                              Incorporator
```

## Filling in the blanks

| Blank | What goes there |
|---|---|
| **SECOND** — registered office and agent | Supplied by the commercial registered agent you retain. Retain them *first*; they give you the exact street address, city, county and zip to use, and most will file the certificate for you as part of the fee. |
| **FIFTH** — incorporator | One person, any adult. The incorporator's only job is to sign this and then appoint the initial board; they need not be a director afterwards. You can be your own incorporator. Use a real mailing address — it becomes public record. |
| **WITNESS** — date and signature | Date of signing, not of filing. |

## Notes on the drafting choices

**"The corporation shall have no members" (Article FOURTH).** Delaware §102(a)(4) requires a
nonstock certificate to state the conditions of membership or refer them to the bylaws. A
non-membership corporation — self-perpetuating board that elects its own successors — is far
simpler to govern and is what nearly all US operating charities use. Adding members means
member meetings, member voting rights and member notice requirements. Do not add members
without a specific reason.

**Article TENTH.** Delaware §102(b)(7) exculpation. Cheap insurance that makes recruiting
volunteer directors materially easier. It does not cover breaches of the duty of loyalty,
bad-faith acts, or improper personal benefit — nor should it.

**What is deliberately absent.** No initial directors are named. Delaware does not require
it, and naming them here means a state filing every time the board changes. The incorporator
appoints the initial board at the organizational meeting (`05`) instead.

## After it is filed

1. Save the stamped certificate and the file number — you need both for the EIN application.
2. Order the certified copy if you have not already.
3. Calendar the **Delaware annual report: due 1 March every year, $25.**
4. Go straight to `06` and get the EIN. Under an hour, and it unblocks everything else.
