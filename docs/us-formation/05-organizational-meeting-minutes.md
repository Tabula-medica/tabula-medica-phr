# 05 · Organizational meeting — minutes and resolutions

Held after the Certificate of Incorporation is filed and, ideally, after the EIN is issued.
This one meeting does eight things at once and is the document your bank, the IRS and every
future funder will ask to see.

It can be held by video call, and the resolutions can alternatively be executed as a
unanimous written consent (`§4.8` of the bylaws) if scheduling is hard — but an actual
meeting with actual minutes is stronger evidence of a functioning board, and you want that
on the record for a first-year organisation.

---

```
                     MINUTES OF THE ORGANIZATIONAL MEETING
                        OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF
                              [[ORG_NAME]]


Date:      ______________, 20____
Time:      ______________
Place:     ______________________ (or by video conference)


1.  CALL TO ORDER

    ____________________, the Incorporator of the Corporation, called the
    meeting to order at ______ and acted as temporary Chair.
    ____________________ was appointed temporary Secretary to record the

    The Incorporator reported that the Certificate of Incorporation of the

    Directors of the Corporation, to serve until their successors are duly
    elected and qualified:

         ____________________________     Term expiring 20____
         ____________________________     Term expiring 20____

    The Incorporator thereupon resigned as Incorporator, the appointment of
    Directors having been completed.


3.  RATIFICATION OF THE CERTIFICATE OF INCORPORATION

    RESOLVED, that the Certificate of Incorporation as filed is hereby
    ratified, approved and adopted, and that the Secretary is directed to
    insert a certified copy in the corporate records.


4.  ADOPTION OF BYLAWS

    The proposed Bylaws were presented and read.

    RESOLVED, that the Bylaws presented to this meeting are adopted as the
    Bylaws of the Corporation, and that the Secretary is directed to insert a
    signed copy in the corporate records.


5.  ELECTION OF OFFICERS

    RESOLVED, that the following persons are elected to the offices set
    opposite their names, to serve until the next annual meeting and until
    their successors are elected and qualified:

         Chair:               ____________________________
         Secretary:           ____________________________
         Treasurer:           ____________________________


6.  ADOPTION OF THE CONFLICT OF INTEREST POLICY

    The proposed Conflict of Interest Policy was presented and read.

    RESOLVED, that the Conflict of Interest Policy presented to this meeting is
    adopted; and

    FURTHER RESOLVED, that each Director and officer shall execute the Annual
    Statement appended to the policy before the close of this meeting and
    annually thereafter.

    Each Director and officer present executed the Annual Statement. The signed
    statements are appended to these minutes.


7.  FISCAL YEAR AND ACCOUNTING

    RESOLVED, that the fiscal year of the Corporation shall end on 31 December
    of each year; and

    FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Corporation shall maintain its books on the cash
    basis of accounting until the Board determines otherwise.


8.  EMPLOYER IDENTIFICATION NUMBER

    RESOLVED, that the officers of the Corporation are authorized and directed
    to apply for an Employer Identification Number from the Internal Revenue
    Service.

    [If already obtained: The Treasurer reported that Employer Identification
    Number ____________ was issued to the Corporation on ______________.]


9.  APPLICATION FOR RECOGNITION OF EXEMPTION

    RESOLVED, that the Corporation shall apply to the Internal Revenue Service
    for recognition of exemption from federal income tax as an organization
    described in Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code, on Form
    1023-EZ; and

    FURTHER RESOLVED, that ____________________ is authorized to execute and
    file the application and to pay the required user fee of $275 on behalf of
    the Corporation; and

    FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Board has determined that the Corporation meets
    the eligibility requirements for Form 1023-EZ, having reviewed the Form
    1023-EZ Eligibility Worksheet, and that the Corporation projects annual
    gross receipts of $50,000 or less in each of its first three years and
    total assets of $250,000 or less.


10. BANKING RESOLUTION

    RESOLVED, that the Corporation shall open one or more accounts at
    ____________________________; and

    FURTHER RESOLVED, that the following persons are authorized signatories on
    such accounts:

         ____________________________
         ____________________________

    FURTHER RESOLVED, that any single disbursement exceeding $______ shall
    require two authorized signatures; and

    FURTHER RESOLVED, that the officers are authorized to execute such
    resolutions and documents as the depository institution may require.


11. ADOPTION OF PROGRAM NAMES

    RESOLVED, that the Corporation shall conduct its charitable and educational
    activities under the following program names, each of which is a trade name
    of the Corporation and not a separate legal entity:

         Katha.kids   — children's stories and literacy
         UHR          — health-record access
         Noorjyoti    — community welfare
         Unraj        — language and cultural heritage

    FURTHER RESOLVED, that the officers are authorized to register such assumed
    or trade names with any governmental authority requiring registration, and
    to apply for registration of the corresponding trademarks.


12. REGISTERED AGENT

    RESOLVED, that ____________________________ is confirmed as the registered
    agent of the Corporation in the State of Delaware, and the officers are
    authorized to pay the annual fee.


13. REIMBURSEMENT OF ORGANIZATIONAL EXPENSES

    RESOLVED, that the Corporation shall reimburse ____________________ for
    expenses of $________ incurred in connection with the formation of the
    Corporation, upon presentation of receipts.


14. CORPORATE RECORDS

    RESOLVED, that the Secretary shall maintain a corporate record book
    containing the Certificate of Incorporation, the Bylaws, the Conflict of
    Interest Policy and executed Annual Statements, minutes of all meetings,
    the Employer Identification Number confirmation, the determination letter
    when received, and copies of all governmental filings.


15. ADJOURNMENT

    There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned at ______.


                              _________________________________
                              Secretary


Approved: ______________, 20____
```

## Order of operations, and the one thing people get wrong

The most common sequencing mistake is filing the 1023-EZ before adopting the bylaws and
conflict-of-interest policy. The form asks whether you have adopted a conflict-of-interest
policy, and it is signed under penalty of perjury. Hold the meeting first. It costs a day.

Second most common: forgetting resolution 11. If a donor writes a cheque to "Katha.kids" and
your bank account is in the legal name with no registered trade name, the cheque bounces
back. Adopt the programme names formally, register them where your state requires, and tell
the bank about them when you open the account.
