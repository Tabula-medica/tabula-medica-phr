# 10 · Day one after the determination letter

The determination letter arrives by post and by IRS online account. **Everything below can
be started the same day**, and the total is roughly $155,000 a year in advertising and
software. Work down the list in order — the first item produces a verification token most of
the others depend on.

Have ready: the determination letter PDF, the EIN, the exact legal name as it appears on the
IRS record, and the live website.

---

## 1. Google for Nonprofits — do this first

`google.com/nonprofits` → Get Started.

You need a Google account **on your own domain** (i.e. `you@yourdomain.org`, not
`@gmail.com`) before enrolling. If you do not have one yet, this is the blocker — set up
email on the domain first.

Google verifies through **Goodstack**. Typical turnaround about a week. Once approved,
activate in this order:

1. **Google Workspace for Nonprofits** — free Business Standard tier. Migrate email here
   immediately; roughly $2,000/year for a ten-person team.
2. **Google Ad Grants** — the $10,000/month one. You will supply the website URL and describe
   intended use. **Configure conversion tracking before your first ad runs** (`09`).
3. **YouTube Nonprofit Program** — donation cards and link features on your channel. Directly
   useful for Katha.kids.
4. **Google Cloud for Nonprofits** — $2,000/year in credits. Relevant: the existing
   application in this repository already deploys to GCP.

## 2. Microsoft for Nonprofits

`microsoft.com/nonprofits`. Microsoft 365 Business Basic free for up to 300 users; Business
Premium at roughly $5.50/user/month against $22 retail; $2,000/year in Azure credits. Worth
claiming even if you standardise on Google Workspace — the Azure credits alone justify it.

## 3. Canva for Nonprofits

`canva.com/nonprofits`. Full premium plan free for up to 50 users. Best effort-to-value ratio
on this entire list: one form, and every programme gets professional design capability. Do
this one even on a busy day.

## 4. TechSoup

`techsoup.org`. Register the organisation and complete validation — you receive a validation
token that several other programmes accept. Unlocks donated and deeply discounted Adobe,
Zoom, Bitdefender, Autodesk and Microsoft licences.

## 5. Candid (formerly GuideStar)

`candid.org` → claim your nonprofit profile. Free, immediate, and the **Seal of Transparency**
feeds Benevity, Bright Funds and most corporate giving portals. Complete it fully — the seal
levels are visible to funders and a bronze seal reads as an abandoned profile.

## 6. Benevity and Goodstack causes listings

Once Goodstack has verified you for Google, you are largely already in their system. Confirm
your listing so corporate matching-gift programmes can find you. Employee matching is free
money that requires only that you be findable.

## 7. Salesforce Power of Us

`salesforce.org`. Ten free licences. Only worth the setup time if you expect real donor-
management needs; otherwise defer — it is a significant configuration project.

## 8. Apple Developer Program — with the fee waiver

`developer.apple.com/support/fee-waiver`

**This is why the US entity matters for the apps.** Apple waives the $99/year fee for
non-profits in eligible regions: Australia, Brazil, Canada, mainland China, France, Germany,
Israel, Italy, Japan, Mexico, South Korea, the UK and the US. **India is not on the list.**

Conditions: you must be a legal entity with non-profit status, not an individual or sole
proprietor; you must distribute **only free apps**; and you must **not** sign the Paid
Applications Agreement or sell digital goods or services through any of your apps.

So enrol the **US 501(c)(3)** as the App Store publisher for Katha.kids and UHR. Select the
fee-waiver option during enrolment — you cannot easily add it afterwards. Note that
enrolment review for non-profits runs slower than the standard path; allow several weeks.

## 9. Google Play

Play Console registration is a one-time $25 and there is no standard non-profit waiver, so
just pay it. What matters more is the policy posture: register Katha.kids under **Designed
for Families**, which requires no third-party ad SDKs and no behavioural advertising —
consistent with COPPA, with the `.kids` registry commitments, and with the Ad Grants
no-ads rule.

## 10. Free earned media

Sign up as a source on **Qwoted**, **Featured** and **SourceBottle**. HARO/Connectively shut
down in December 2024 — do not go looking for it. AmazonSmile ended in February 2023 — same.

---

## Sequencing note

Items 1 and 4 produce verification credentials the others consume, so do them first. Items
3 and 5 take under thirty minutes each and pay off immediately. Item 8 has the longest
review queue — start it early even though it is last in value order.

## Then: the thing that actually determines whether any of this worked

Everything above is one-time setup. The Ad Grant is the only item with an ongoing
requirement, and it is the one worth $120,000 a year. Put a recurring four-hour block in the
calendar every month for account management before you do anything else, and treat the 5%
click-through rate as a live operational metric rather than a compliance footnote.

The rest of the stack you claim once and keep. The Ad Grant you keep only by working it.
