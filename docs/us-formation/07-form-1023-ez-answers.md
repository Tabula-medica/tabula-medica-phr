# 07 · Form 1023-EZ — field-by-field

**Fee:** $275, paid on Pay.gov at the time of filing (Rev. Proc. 2025-5).
**Filed at:** `pay.gov` — search "1023-EZ". You cannot file it anywhere else, and you need a
free Pay.gov account first.
**Typical decision:** 2–6 weeks.
**Retroactivity:** exemption is effective from the date of incorporation as long as you file
within **27 months** of the end of the month in which you incorporated. You will be filing
within weeks, so this is comfortable — but do not let it drift.

> IRS pages could not be fetched directly from this environment, so **check every field
> against the live form and the current Instructions for Form 1023-EZ before submitting.**
> The January 2025 revision modified worksheet question 29 and added questions 31–34, so the
> worksheet is longer than older guides show.

## Before you start: the Eligibility Worksheet

You must complete the worksheet in the instructions. **You do not submit it** — you retain
it. But you attest that you completed it, and answering "Yes" to any question disqualifies
you from the EZ.

The disqualifiers that could plausibly apply to this organisation:

| Worksheet topic | Your answer | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Projected annual gross receipts exceeding **$50,000** in any of the next 3 years | **No** — this is the decision you made | If donations take off faster than expected, you do not retroactively lose exemption, but you will move to full Form 990 filing and should file a full 1023 if you ever restructure |
| Total assets exceeding **$250,000** | **No** | |
| Formed under the laws of a **foreign country** | **No** | Delaware. This is the foreign-related disqualifier, and it is about where the *entity* is formed, not where it operates |
| Are you an **LLC** | **No** | Nonstock corporation |
| Successor to a **for-profit entity** | **No** | ⚠️ Answer this carefully. If any programme, code base or asset is being transferred in from an existing for-profit company, the answer may be Yes, and Yes disqualifies you from the EZ. See the warning below. |
| Applying as a **church, school or hospital** | **No** | UHR provides tools to patients; it is not a hospital or medical care provider |
| **Private foundation** classification | **No** — you are applying as a public charity | |

**⚠️ The successor question is the one to think hard about.** If the UHR software, the
Katha.kids content library, or any other asset currently sits inside a for-profit entity and
is being contributed to the charity, you may be a "successor to a for-profit entity" for
worksheet purposes, which puts you on the full Form 1023. Being a successor is not
disqualifying in substance — it just cannot be handled on the EZ. Work out the asset
provenance before you answer, and if any part of it came from a company, talk to the
attorney. Getting this wrong is the fastest route to a revoked exemption.

**Parts II.6 through II.8 are the whole reason `02` is drafted the way it is.** These are
attestations under penalty of perjury that the clauses already exist in your filed
certificate. Have the certificate open in front of you when you check these boxes, and
confirm the language is actually there. If you filed Delaware's short exempt-corporation
form instead of the long-form text, **the honest answer to all three is No** and you must
amend the certificate before proceeding.

### On Part III.8 — foreign activities

Answer this truthfully. Conducting activities outside the United States is **not** a
disqualifier for the 1023-EZ (the disqualifier is being *formed* outside the US), and
checking "Yes" does not sink the application.

But given you have chosen to sequence India second, the cleanest posture for year one is
that the US entity conducts its programmes in the US and makes **no foreign grants**. Then
this is a straightforward "No" and there is nothing to explain.

**When India comes back on the agenda, this is the trigger point.** Before the US entity
sends a single dollar to the Indian Section 8 company, you need expenditure-responsibility
procedures in place, and you should get advice on whether your facts have outgrown the EZ.
Note it in the corporate record now so it is not forgotten.

## Part IV — Foundation Classification

| Field | Answer |
|---|---|
| Foundation classification | **509(a)(1) and 170(b)(1)(A)(vi)** — an organization that normally receives a substantial part of its support from a governmental unit or from the general public |

This is the right box for an organisation funded by donations from many small donors. It is
also the classification that Google, Benevity and most corporate matching-gift portals
expect to see. Do not check private foundation.

If your funding will come predominantly from fees for exempt-function services rather than
donations, 509(a)(2) is the alternative — but for this organisation, whose services are all
free, 509(a)(1) is correct.

## Immediately after filing

1. Save the Pay.gov confirmation and payment receipt to the corporate records.
2. Note the submission date. If you have heard nothing after **90 days**, call IRS Exempt
   Organizations Customer Account Services on 877-829-5500 and ask for status.
3. **Start building the website now if it is not already live** (`09`). The determination
   letter is worthless to you until there is a site for Google to review, and the site is
   the longest-lead item you fully control.
4. Do not wait for the letter to open the bank account or start programme work. You are a
   valid Delaware non-profit corporation from the incorporation date; the determination
   merely recognises federal exemption, retroactively.
