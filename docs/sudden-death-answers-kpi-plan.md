## Core metrics to track from case #1

| Metric | Definition | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Cases per month | Completed evaluations (consent through report delivered) | Core volume/growth indicator |
| Resolution rate achieved | % of cases with a probable or definite cause found, split by tier (genetics-only vs. full Complete package) | Must track against the published 13-30% definite-genetic-cause range — if actual results run meaningfully below that, marketing claims need revisiting (see `docs/sudden-death-answers-website-copy.md` honest-expectations section) |
| SLA adherence | % of cases meeting each stage SLA (see `docs/sudden-death-answers-ops-playbook.md`) | Operational health |
| Cascade-testing conversion | % of cases with a positive genetic finding where at least one relative completes cascade testing | Core to the family-risk value proposition, and a secondary revenue stream |
| Family NPS / satisfaction | Post-case survey, ideally 2-4 weeks after report delivery (not immediately, given the grief context) | Referral quality and word-of-mouth in diaspora communities are the primary growth channel |
| Referral source mix | % from funeral homes vs. hospitals vs. direct/word-of-mouth | Tells us which partnership investments are working |
| Cost per case (actual vs. ~$910 fully-loaded target) | Actual wholesale + ops cost per completed case | Margin tracking against the pricing tiers |
| ME/coroner assertion rate | % of cases where the ME/coroner later asserted jurisdiction after evaluation started | Regulatory risk indicator — should be near zero if the jurisdiction decision tree (checklist item J) is working |

## Reporting cadence
- **Weekly (internal ops):** case volume, SLA adherence, any escalations.
- **Monthly (leadership):** full KPI table above, cost-per-case trend, metro #1 vs. metro #2 comparison once both are live.
- **Quarterly:** resolution-rate trend vs. published range, NPS trend, regulatory/FDA-LDT status recheck (checklist item F).

## Early warning thresholds (flag for review, not necessarily failure)
- Resolution rate more than 5 points below the published range for two consecutive months.
- SLA adherence below 80% on any stage for two consecutive weeks.
- Any ME/coroner jurisdiction assertion after evaluation has started — review the jurisdiction decision tree and intake script immediately.
