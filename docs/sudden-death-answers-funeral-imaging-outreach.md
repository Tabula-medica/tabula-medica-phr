## 1. Funeral home partnership outreach (item D)

**Target profile:** funeral homes serving South Asian (Hindu, Muslim, Sikh), Orthodox Jewish, or Chinese communities with same-day/24-48h burial or cremation norms, in the launch metro.

**Outreach email:**

> Subject: Partnership inquiry — non-invasive cause-of-death evaluation for your families
>
> Hello [Name],
>
> TabulaMedica offers "Sudden Death Answers," a non-invasive service that helps families understand a sudden, unexplained death without a traditional autopsy — using a scan, a simple blood/cheek-swab genetic test, and toxicology, all completed within the window before burial or cremation.
>
> We'd like to explore a referral partnership: when your families are grieving a sudden death and want answers but are declining autopsy for religious or personal reasons, we can be an option you offer them. There's no cost or obligation to your funeral home — we handle the family conversation, consent, sample collection, and coordination directly, working around your existing timeline.
>
> Could we set up a short call to explain the service and see if it's a fit for the families you serve?
>
> Best,
> [Name / Title], TabulaMedica

**Discussion points for the call:**
- How collection logistics work at their facility (space, timing, who's present).
- Referral process: how a warm handoff happens without funeral home staff needing to explain the science.
- No referral fees implied or offered — keep this a values-aligned partnership, not a kickback arrangement (flag for counsel if any compensation model is considered later).

## 2. Imaging partner outreach (item I, feeds item X)

**Target profile:** radiology group or mobile-CT provider able to perform postmortem CT with targeted coronary angiography (PMCTA), with after-hours/weekend read capacity.

**Outreach email:**

> Subject: Partnership inquiry — postmortem CT/CTA imaging, [metro]
>
> Hello [Name],
>
> TabulaMedica is launching a non-invasive postmortem evaluation service for sudden unexplained adult death in [metro]. We need an imaging partner able to perform postmortem CT, ideally with targeted coronary angiography (PMCTA), within 24-48 hours of first contact — including nights and weekends, since sudden deaths don't happen on a schedule.
>
> We'd like to discuss:
> - Your capacity and pricing for postmortem CT/CTA studies
> - Radiologist read turnaround, including after-hours coverage
> - Whether studies would be done at your facility, ours, or via mobile unit
> - Any prior experience with postmortem or forensic imaging
>
> Could we schedule a call this week or next?
>
> Best,
> [Name / Title], TabulaMedica

**Decision criteria:** read turnaround against the 24-48h SLA, after-hours coverage, price, and any postmortem imaging track record (forensic or hospital-based).
