## What we need counsel for

TabulaMedica is launching "Sudden Death Answers," a non-invasive postmortem molecular autopsy service (genetic panel + postmortem CT/CTA + needle-only toxicology + records review), positioned as an alternative to traditional autopsy for sudden unexplained adult death. We need counsel experienced in:

1. **CLIA reference-lab structures** — we will operate as a marketer/coordinator using outside CLIA-certified reference labs (Labcorp/Invitae, GeneDx, Blueprint Genetics), not our own lab. Need review of the legal structure for this model, including whether we need our own CLIA registration for any specimen-handling step.
2. **LDT (laboratory-developed test) regulatory status** — the FDA's LDT rule was vacated (31 Mar 2025, E.D. Tex., *ACLA v. FDA*), and FDA did not appeal; the Enhancing CLIA Act of 2026 is pending in Congress. Need current-status confirmation and monitoring plan before we finalize the lab-partner contracts.
3. **Medical examiner / coroner jurisdiction** — need a state-by-state (starting with our two launch metros) summary of when an ME/coroner must be notified or can assert jurisdiction over a death we are evaluating, and what happens if they assert it after we've started.
4. **Next-of-kin / consent authority** — state-by-state hierarchy for who can authorize postmortem sample collection and records release absent an advance directive.
5. **HIPAA decedent rules** — confirm the 50-year post-death PHI protection period (45 CFR 160.103) and how it applies to our data-sharing with reference labs, imaging partners, and the TabulaMedica PHR platform.
6. **GINA gaps** — confirm and help us word the disclosure that GINA does not cover life, disability, or long-term-care insurance underwriting.
7. **Review of draft consent forms** — see `docs/sudden-death-answers-consent-forms.md` (four forms: next-of-kin authorization, ME/coroner coordination, honest resolution-rate disclosure, GINA disclosure).
8. **Vendor BAAs** — review/negotiate Business Associate Agreements with reference labs, imaging partners, couriers, and confirm our own BAA obligations as a covered entity or business associate (need counsel's view on which we are here).

## Scope of engagement requested
- Initial regulatory memo covering items 1-6 above, focused on our two launch metros (see `docs/sudden-death-answers-launch-metro-shortlist.md`).
- Review and redline of the four consent forms.
- Review of BAA templates once received from lab/imaging vendors.
- Ongoing availability for questions as we finalize vendor contracts.
