## Kit contents

| Item | Purpose | Sourcing note |
|---|---|---|
| Buccal swab (2x, sterile) | Genetic sample collection | Standard forensic/clinical buccal swab kit |
| Dried blood spot (FTA) card | Backup/primary genetic sample, stable at room temp | Confirm compatibility with selected reference lab (see RFP tracking table) |
| Sample labels (pre-printed barcode + manual fields) | Chain-of-custody | Name, DOB, date/time of death, date/time of collection, collector initials |
| Chain-of-custody form (paper, carbon-copy) | Legal/forensic traceability | One copy stays with family/funeral home, one travels with sample |
| Biohazard specimen bags (2x) | Transport safety | Standard clinical biohazard bag |
| Insulated courier pouch | Sample stability in transit | Confirm temperature requirements with lab (buccal/FTA are typically room-stable; toxicology specimens may need cold chain — confirm separately) |
| Consent form packet (printed) | See `docs/sudden-death-answers-consent-forms.md` | Include large-type honest-resolution-rate disclosure as a separate initialed page |
| Instruction card for collector | Standardize technique | One-page, numbered steps, photos if possible |
| Pre-paid return courier label | Logistics | Match to lab's preferred courier (FedEx/UPS medical) |

## Collection instruction card (draft)

1. Confirm consent forms signed and initialed (all four forms) before opening kit.
2. Put on gloves.
3. Buccal swab: rub firmly against inner cheek for 15 seconds, both sides, using separate swabs.
4. Dried blood spot: follow card manufacturer's fill-volume instructions exactly (under/overfilling invalidates the sample).
5. Label every sample immediately — never batch-label at the end.
6. Complete chain-of-custody form in full, both copies.
7. Seal in biohazard bag, place in insulated courier pouch.
8. Photograph completed labels and chain-of-custody form for the case file before sealing.
9. Schedule courier pickup same day.

## QA checklist before first live kit use
- [ ] 10 dummy-run kits assembled and inventoried for completeness.
- [ ] Collector (whoever will do this — internal staff, contracted phlebotomist/mortuary tech) trained on the instruction card and observed doing one supervised dry run.
- [ ] Confirm with selected reference lab: exact swab/FTA card brand accepted, minimum fill volume, and any rejection criteria.
- [ ] Confirm courier chain from launch metro to lab, including worst-case transit time, against sample stability window.
- [ ] Confirm biohazard disposal/handling compliance with local regulations for the collector.

**Open dependency:** finalize once a reference lab is selected (checklist item C) — swab/FTA brand and fill requirements vary by lab.
