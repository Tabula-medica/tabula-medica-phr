# Sudden Death Answers — Launch Metro Shortlist

**Covers checklist item L** in `docs/sudden-death-answers-az-todo.md`.
**Status:** Recommendation based on known US diaspora population density; needs local confirmation of funeral-home and imaging-partner availability before finalizing (see below).

---

## Recommended two launch metros

### 1. New York / New Jersey metro (esp. Edison/Iselin/Jersey City NJ, Queens NY)
- Largest US concentration of South Asian (Indian, Pakistani, Bangladeshi) population; also large Orthodox Jewish communities (Brooklyn) with same-day burial norms.
- Deep bench of funeral homes already serving these communities — partnership outreach should be efficient.
- Dense hospital/imaging infrastructure; multiple academic medical centers with postmortem CT capability or radiology groups that could be contracted for mobile/on-site scanning.
- Con: highest cost of doing business (staff, courier, real estate if any local presence is needed).

### 2. Chicago metro (or Houston as an alternative)
- **Chicago:** substantial South Asian population (Devon Avenue corridor), sizable Orthodox Jewish community, established diaspora funeral-home network; more moderate cost base than NY/NJ.
- **Houston alternative:** large and fast-growing South Asian and Pakistani population, strong hospital/imaging system (Texas Medical Center), lower cost base; worth comparing directly against Chicago once funeral-home partner conversations start.

## Why not other candidates (for the record)
- **Bay Area:** large South Asian population but very high cost base and imaging/logistics vendor pricing; keep on the list for phase 2.
- **Dallas-Fort Worth:** fast-growing diaspora population, could be phase 2 alongside Bay Area.
- **Southern California (LA/Orange County/Artesia):** large diaspora population, worth phase 2 consideration; deprioritized for launch only because NY/NJ and Chicago/Houston give tighter geographic focus for the first 120 days.

## What still needs local confirmation before finalizing
1. At least 3-5 funeral homes per metro willing to be launch partners and able to support 24h sample-collection logistics (checklist item D).
2. At least one imaging partner (radiology group or mobile CT vendor) per metro confirmed able to do postmortem CT/CTA within the 24-48h SLA, including night/weekend coverage (checklist item X).
3. State-specific ME/coroner jurisdiction rules for NY, NJ, IL (and TX if Houston is chosen) — feed into counsel's regulatory memo (checklist item R).

**Recommendation:** proceed with NY/NJ as metro #1 immediately (largest, most established diaspora funeral-home network). Run Chicago vs. Houston as a fast comparison — reach out to 2-3 funeral homes and one imaging group in each over the next two weeks, then decide metro #2 based on actual partner responsiveness, not population data alone.
