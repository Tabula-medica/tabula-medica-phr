# Sudden Death Answers — A-to-Z Launch To-Do List

**Project:** Sudden Death Answers — Non-Invasive Evaluation (TabulaMedica PHR service line)
**Scope:** Molecular autopsy + PMCT/PMCTA + needle-only toxicology + records review, positioned as a non-invasive alternative to autopsy for sudden unexplained adult death.
**Source:** Builds on `docs/molecular-autopsy-service-launch-brief.md`.
**Status:** Pre-launch checklist. Check off items as they close; each maps to an owner and a real launch dependency, not a placeholder.

- [ ] **A — Authorization & consent forms.** Draft next-of-kin/executor consent, ME-release consent, and the "honest resolution rate" disclosure (13-30% definite genetic cause) for legal review.
- [ ] **B — Budget approval.** Lock the $220k-365k asset-light launch budget; get sign-off on the first 120 days of spend before any lab BAA is signed.
- [ ] **C — CLIA lab contracts.** Get wholesale quotes and Business Associate Agreements from Labcorp/Invitae, GeneDx, and Blueprint Genetics; select primary + backup reference lab.
- [ ] **D — Diaspora channel partnerships.** Identify funeral homes serving Indian/Pakistani/Bangladeshi/Chinese/Orthodox Jewish communities in the two launch metros; confirm same-day/24h collection logistics.
- [ ] **E — Embalming-window logistics.** Finalize the collection kit and courier SOP so buccal/blood-spot samples and PMCT scans happen within the ~24-72h pre-embalming/cremation window.
- [ ] **F — FDA/LDT status check.** Confirm current status of the vacated LDT rule and the Enhancing CLIA Act before finalizing the reference-lab model; re-check quarterly.
- [ ] **G — GINA gap disclosure.** Add life-insurance GINA-gap language to the consent and genetic-counseling scripts.
- [ ] **H — HIPAA decedent policy.** Document the 50-year decedent PHI protection rule (45 CFR 160.103) in the data-handling policy and train intake staff on it.
- [ ] **I — Imaging partner (PMCT/PMCTA).** Contract a radiology group or mobile-CT provider capable of postmortem CT with targeted coronary angiography in each launch metro.
- [ ] **J — Jurisdiction decision tree.** Publish the three-branch ME/coroner jurisdiction flow (declines case / releases without autopsy / retains case) as a one-page staff reference.
- [ ] **K — Kit design and QA.** Finalize the physical collection kit (buccal swab, dried blood spot/FTA card, labels, chain-of-custody form) and run a QA pass on 10 dummy kits before go-live.
- [ ] **L — Launch metro selection.** Pick the first two metros based on diaspora density, funeral-home density, and imaging-partner availability.
- [ ] **M — Marketing copy localization.** Translate core consumer copy into Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Punjabi, Urdu, and Simplified Chinese; route through native-speaker review, not machine translation alone.
- [ ] **N — Next-of-kin outreach script.** Write the compassionate, non-pressuring intake script funeral-home staff or on-call coordinators use in the first phone call.
- [ ] **O — Ops playbook for on-call coordination.** Define the 24/7 on-call rotation and SLA (sample collection dispatched within X hours of first contact).
- [ ] **P — Pricing finalization.** Confirm the four package tiers (Core $1,995 / Core+Tox $2,495 / Complete $3,995-4,495 / Cascade $299) against actual wholesale costs once lab contracts close.
- [ ] **Q — Quality control on turnaround.** Set and monitor SLAs: PMCT read in 24-48h, toxicology in 1-2 weeks, genetics in 2-6 weeks; build a dashboard to track misses.
- [ ] **R — Regulatory/legal counsel retained.** Engage health-law counsel experienced in CLIA reference-lab structures and ME/coroner authorization rules before accepting the first case.
- [ ] **S — Sample chain-of-custody.** Build the tracking system (barcode or manual log) from collection through lab receipt, matching HIPAA and forensic-evidence standards.
- [ ] **T — TabulaMedica PHR integration.** Wire the family graph, legacy vault, and ICD-10 codes (R99, R96.0, I46.1, R95) so results and records review flow into the existing PHR product.
- [ ] **U — Underwriting the honest-yield message.** Pressure-test the marketing claims against the published diagnostic-yield literature (13-30% P/LP in autopsy-negative sudden death under 40) so nothing overstates resolution odds.
- [ ] **V — Vendor BAAs and data flows.** Execute Business Associate Agreements with every vendor touching PHI (labs, imaging, courier, PHR platform) before the first live case.
- [ ] **W — Website/intake page build.** Stand up the public-facing page explaining the service, pricing, and how to request it, in English plus the priority launch languages.
- [ ] **X — X-ray/CT read capacity.** Confirm the imaging partner has radiologist coverage (including nights/weekends) to read PMCT/PMCTA within the 24-48h SLA — sudden deaths don't wait for business hours.
- [ ] **Y — Year-one metrics plan.** Define the KPIs to track from day one: cases per month, resolution rate achieved vs. published range, NPS from families, cascade-testing conversion.
- [ ] **Z — Zero-defect launch review.** Run a pre-launch tabletop exercise (mock case end-to-end) covering consent, collection, lab, imaging, and results delivery before accepting the first real family.
